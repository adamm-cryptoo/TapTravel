How it works

Tap a place

Zoom the world map and tap any open gate. You see local time, language, and what to do there.

Press Teleport

Confirm your gate and your Local Look. The app clears your arrival spot and counts you down.

Step out

You arrive at the gate in about a second. Tap Return any time to come home.

Local Look: arrive dressed for the place

When you teleport, TapTravel changes your clothing, hairstyle, accessories, and spoken language to fit your destination. In Barcelona you get linen and sunglasses. In Tokyo you get tailored layers and fluent Japanese.

Your face, age, gender, skin tone, and body never change. Local Look is optional, and you can switch it off or revert it at any time.
