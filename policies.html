Legal ages

AgeWhat you can do
Under 13No account. TapTravel is not available.
13 to 17Junior account on a Family plan only. A parent approves every gate, and arrivals end by 9 pm local time.
18 and overAny plan, all gates your plan unlocks, and solo teleports.
At your destinationLocal law applies the moment you arrive, including age limits for drinking, driving, and entry.

Local Look rules

  • Local Look is opt-in and you can switch it off before or after any teleport.
  • It changes clothing, hairstyle, accessories, and spoken language only.
  • It never changes your face, age, gender, skin tone, ethnicity, or body.
  • It cannot copy a real person or create a disguise for fraud or evasion.
  • Your own look returns automatically when you return home.

Safety and arrival

  • Every gate is a staffed, cleared public space.
  • The Return button is always on screen and brings you home within seconds.
  • Parents of Junior accounts get arrival and return alerts.

Privacy

  • We store your gate history to run your account and help you return.
  • We do not sell your location or identity data.
  • You can export or delete your data from your account at any time.

Not allowed

  • Teleporting into private property or restricted areas.
  • Carrying weapons, hazardous materials, or items banned at your destination.
  • Using TapTravel to avoid law enforcement or cross borders without required documents.

Billing and refunds

  • Cancel any time. Your plan runs until the end of the period you paid for.
  • New paid subscribers get a full refund within 14 days if they used fewer than 3 teleports.
  • Black Friday annual discounts apply to the first year only.
